# Valoon

**Smart communication software for construction and field work.** Valoon turns
WhatsApp, Teams, and other messaging apps your teams already use into a
structured project management platform — no new app for the field, no training,
no friction.

Learn more at [valoon.chat](https://valoon.chat/).

## What we do

Construction sites, trades, and facility management teams run on chat. Valoon
bridges the office and the job site by organizing that chatter into projects,
archiving photos and documentation, generating timesheets and reports, and
translating messages across 50+ languages in real time — all while workers keep
using WhatsApp exactly as they always have.

- **3,000+ construction projects** across **10 countries**
- **German-hosted** infrastructure — ISO 27001 and SOC 2 Type 2 certified
- **Legally-secure** (beweissichere) documentation built in
- **Free for WhatsApp users** — only office seats are billed
